Privacy Policy

Don't Shout is designed to work locally. The developer does not collect data from the app.

Data collection

Don't Shout does not actively transmit or collect your photos, recordings, imported audio, reminders, custom lines, favorites, feedback, or pause history. The app does not include accounts, advertising, analytics, third-party tracking SDKs, social features, AI analysis, or developer-operated cloud storage.

Data stored on your device

A photo or quiet background, reminders, recordings, imported audio, scene choices, custom lines, favorites, feedback, and pause events are stored locally by default. If system-level iCloud or device backup is enabled, local app data may be included according to Apple's backup settings. The app itself does not upload this data to the developer.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only when you choose to record a local pause sound.
  • Photo picker or camera: only when you choose a photo for your pause kit. The app does not request unrestricted photo-library access.
  • Files: only when you choose to import a local audio file.
  • Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode: only to protect private areas and confirm deletion. The emergency pause flow remains available.

Delete local data

You can clear your local pause kit, text, recordings, imported audio, favorites, feedback, and event history from Settings inside the app.

Children and health

Don't Shout is for parents and caregivers, not children. It does not provide medical diagnosis, therapy, crisis intervention, or emergency services.
