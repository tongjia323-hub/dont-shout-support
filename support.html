Official app support

Support

Quick answers for setup, local data, privacy controls, and the 30-second pause flow.

Contact support

Email tongjia323@gmail.com for help with Don't Shout. Include your app version, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send passwords, private recordings, or photos of children.

Start a pause

Open Don't Shout and tap the main pause button. The countdown, return line, repair line, and safety guidance remain available without an account, internet connection, payment screen, or biometric lock.

Photo, sound, and reminder

Build a private pause kit with one photo or a quiet background, one reminder, and one sound. You can use a built-in sound, record your own, or import a local audio file. These materials stay on the device by default.

Privacy controls

Private pause mode hides your photo during the pause flow. The material library and settings can be protected with Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode. Clear local data from Settings when needed.

Common fixes

  • If sound is muted, check device volume and Silent Mode.
  • If recording is unavailable, review microphone access in iOS Settings.
  • If a photo is missing, choose it again from the system photo picker.
  • If imported audio will not play, try a standard audio file longer than half a second and under 50 MB.

Safety note

Don't Shout is not a medical, therapy, or crisis intervention service. If you are concerned that you may harm a child, yourself, or someone else, first ensure immediate safety and contact local emergency services or qualified professional support.
